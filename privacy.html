1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the developer of Maplestory Tracker ("we", "us" or "our") handles information when you use the Maplestory Tracker desktop application (the "App"). It is part of our Terms of Use.

Our data collection and processing while you use the App are governed by this policy. This policy is between you and us, not with Overwolf Ltd. We are the controller of the data described in it. Where Overwolf or its partners process data for their own purposes (for example, to serve ads), their own privacy policies apply, as explained in section 5.

2. What the App reads on your screen

To track your progress, the App captures the MapleStory Classic World game window, the same way a screen recorder or screen-sharing tool does, and reads numbers the game shows: EXP, level, mesos, quickslot counts, item pickup messages and the minimap. This happens in memory on your computer. The App does not send these pictures or readings to us or anyone else.

To find the game, the App looks at the titles of the windows open on your computer (the same list a screen-share picker shows). It only looks for the game's title. It does not store or send the titles of other windows.

3. What stays on your computer

The App saves the following in its data folder on your computer (%APPDATA%\Maplestory Tracker). None of it is sent to us.

  • Session history: times, EXP, mesos, kills, potions used, item pickups, level changes, and any name you give a session.
  • Maps: map names and small pictures of the minimap, so the App can recognize maps you visit again.
  • Settings: window positions, hotkeys, item prices and similar choices.
  • Diagnostic log: a technical log that helps fix problems. It stays on your computer unless you choose to send it to us.

You can delete this data at any time: delete sessions in the App's History window, delete the data folder, or uninstall the App.

4. Usage statistics (optional)

The App asks you once whether to send anonymous usage statistics. Nothing is sent unless you say yes. You can change your answer at any time from the App's tray icon menu. If you say no, anything waiting to be sent is deleted.

Usage statistics never include your character name, anything from your screen, names or text you type, file paths, or any other personal information. We use them only to understand which features are used and to improve the App.

They are sent over an encrypted connection to a Google Apps Script web app and stored in a Google Sheet that only we can access. Google processes them for us as a service provider. Like any web server, Google receives your IP address to deliver the request, but the App does not send it to us and we do not store it.

5. Ads, updates and Overwolf

The App is built on Overwolf's platform (ow-electron) and is distributed and updated through Overwolf Ltd. ("Overwolf").

Ads

The App is free and shows ads in its side windows. The ads are provided by Overwolf and its advertising partners. To show, measure and personalize ads and prevent fraud, Overwolf and its partners may collect advertising-related data from your device, such as your IP address, device and advertising identifiers, browser and device information, and how you interact with ads. We share this advertising-related data with Overwolf through the Overwolf ad software in the App. Overwolf and its partners process it under their own privacy policies, including Overwolf's privacy policy. Where the law requires your consent for personalized ads, it is asked for through Overwolf's consent tools.

Updates and platform data

The App checks Overwolf's update servers when it starts and every few hours, to download new versions. Overwolf's software also sends technical information to Overwolf, such as install and uninstall events, the App's ID and version, and an anonymous machine identifier, so that Overwolf can deliver, secure and measure the App. These servers receive your IP address as part of each request.

6. Item links

If you click an item in the App, the App sends that item's name to MeowDB to find its page, and opens the page in your web browser. This only happens when you click. MeowDB is a third-party website with its own privacy practices.

7. What we do not collect

  • The App has no accounts. We do not collect your name, email address, game login, password or payment details.
  • The App does not use single sign-on, so we do not collect or share any email address or single sign-on information with Overwolf.
  • The App does not read the game's memory or files, or any other program's memory or files.
  • We do not sell personal data.

9. Who we share data with

  • Overwolf Ltd.: advertising-related data and the platform data described in section 5.
  • Overwolf's advertising partners: advertising-related data, through Overwolf's ad software.

We do not share data with anyone else, and we do not sell it.

10. How long we keep it

Data on your computer stays until you delete it or uninstall the App. We keep usage statistics for up to 24 months, then delete them. Overwolf and its partners keep the data they handle according to their own policies.

11. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to or restrict how it is used, to receive a copy of it, and to withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect what was done before).

  • Usage statistics: turn them off from the tray icon menu.
  • Delete statistics already sent: because they are anonymous, send us your installation ID, found in analytics.json in the App's data folder, and we will delete them.
  • Data on your computer: delete it in the App, delete the data folder, or uninstall the App.
  • Ads: use Overwolf's consent choices, or contact Overwolf about the ad data it holds.

To use any of these rights, email us at zappah@overwolf.com. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

12. Children

The App is not meant for children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, email us and we will delete it.

13. Security and international transfers

Usage statistics are sent over encrypted connections and stored with access limited to us. Google, Overwolf and their partners may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States and Israel. Where the law requires it, these transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.
