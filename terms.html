2. What the App does

The App is a companion tool for MapleStory Classic World. While the game runs, it looks at the game's window on your screen, the same way a screen recorder or screen-sharing tool would, and reads numbers the game already shows (such as EXP, level, mesos, the quickslot and the minimap). It uses them to show your progress, rates and session history in its own windows.

The App does not change the game, inject anything into it, draw inside it, read or write the game's memory, read the game's files, or send keyboard or mouse input to it. The numbers it shows are estimates based on what was readable on screen, and they can be wrong.
